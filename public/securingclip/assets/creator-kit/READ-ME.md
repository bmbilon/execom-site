# Securing Clip — Creator review kit

## Direction
Stay connected. Stay in flow.

A product-first 24-second concept film and a focused landing page with a simulated checkout. The original reel was about 34 seconds. This revision introduces the product immediately, then presents the mechanism as Enclose, Connect, Secure.

## Included
- Final 24-second 1080p film with original electronic score
- Silent 10-second product loop
- Film poster and storyboard
- Three full-resolution photoreal product concept images
- Original score WAV
- Image generation prompts

The film uses animated camera moves over photoreal concept stills; it is not a mechanical CAD simulation. Imagery was generated with built-in ImageGen using the original product geometry as reference.

## Concept details
A gold-colored, hinged 90-degree enclosure surrounds a ribbed cable liner. A gray screw clamp secures the assembly at the laptop edge. Final fit, specifications, materials and production details are still to be established.

## Checkout demonstration
The proposed CAD $29 single kit and CAD $49 duo are illustrative options. The checkout is a local simulation. No payment is collected, no email is sent, no reservation is made and nothing ships.
