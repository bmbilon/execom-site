## Editorial boundaries

Service descriptions explain potential scope, deliverables, and intake requirements. They do not promise regulatory approval, grant eligibility, patent grants, or financial outcomes. Specialist work is described as coordination where appropriate. Related project links are examples to explore, not claims that every listed deliverable was completed for that project. No private contacts, supplier identities, prices, or credentials are published in the new service copy.

Physical and digital prototyping are separate destinations. Search includes common aliases such as POC, SR&ED/SRED, CAD, APIs, e-commerce, and AI. Technical homepage project disclosures remain collapsed.
